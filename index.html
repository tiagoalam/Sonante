THE IDEA BEHIND SONANTE

A collection is more
than a list of tracks.

It is the albums you return to, the editions you compare, and the discoveries waiting in your own library. Sonante is built around that way of listening.

Browse across your sources, build a queue, and settle into an album. Choose a shared system output or a selected ALSA device. Keep your collection organized with tools that put review and control in your hands.

Before you install

Linux x86_64, GTK 3, WebKitGTK 4.1 API (library version 2.40 or newer), ALSA and OpenSSL 3 are required. The complete installers include the pinned private audio runtimes. Shared EQ requires PipeWire and an available stereo output. Compatibility depends on your distribution, dependencies and hardware; see the Linux documentation for requirements.
